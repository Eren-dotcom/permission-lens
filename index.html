🔐 Permission Lens

Know what can access your NEAR account — before you trust it with more money.

Read-only. We never ask for a seed phrase or private key, and nothing you type here is stored or sent anywhere except the public NEAR network.

Try:

Before you remove any key

  • Removing a key is done in your wallet or with NEAR CLI — Permission Lens cannot remove keys, by design.
  • Never remove your only full-access key. First make sure you have your seed phrase or another full-access key you control, or you can lock yourself out.
  • Start with app keys for apps you no longer use. Check the contract name on the card against the app you remember using.
  • If you do not recognise a full-access key, move funds to a new account first, then sort out the old one.
